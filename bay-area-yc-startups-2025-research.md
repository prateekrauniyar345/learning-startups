## Selection method

1. Start with YC S25 (2025) companies.
2. Keep companies whose public listing says San Francisco, Oakland, Mountain View, or San Francisco Bay Area.
3. Prefer YC’s own directory for product/location descriptions; use Seedtable for batch, location, and reported total funding; use company or reputable news announcements for named investors and later rounds.
4. Do not infer a funding amount, investor, revenue, or traction metric from a logo, hiring page, or marketing claim.

## What stands out

The cohort is heavily concentrated in AI infrastructure, developer tools, enterprise automation, and vertical AI. The public funding distribution is highly skewed: a small group reports multi-million-dollar seed/Series A totals, while most newly accepted companies disclose only the standard YC investment or no public amount. That is normal for private startups and means “not publicly disclosed” should not be read as “unfunded.”

The clearest publicly documented co-investor examples in this set are Dedalus Labs (Kindred Ventures and Saga Ventures), while many other entries have only YC-level evidence in the sources reviewed. The companies with the strongest externally visible funding signals are Wafer, Flai, Halluminate, Idler, Veritus, Design Arena, AgentMail, Manufact, The Prompting Company, Freya, GETASAP, Golpo AI, Burnt, Blue, Dedalus Labs, and Orange Slice.
