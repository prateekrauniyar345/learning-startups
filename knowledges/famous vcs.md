### Prominent Silicon Valley Venture‑Capital Firms (2024‑2025)

Below are the most widely‑recognized VC firms that are actively investing in early‑stage and growth‑stage startups out of Silicon Valley. They are renowned for the size of their funds, the success of their portfolio companies, and their influence on the tech ecosystem.

- **Sequoia Capital** – One of the oldest and most successful firms.  AUM ≈ $55 B; famous exits include Apple, Google, WhatsApp, Zoom, and Stripe.  
- **Andreessen Horowitz (a16z)** – Known for deep‑tech and software bets.  AUM ≈ $42 B (2024); notable portfolio: Facebook, Airbnb, Coinbase, GitHub, Slack.  
- **Accel** – Early‑stage specialist with a strong presence in consumer & enterprise SaaS.  Portfolio highlights: Dropbox, Atlassian, Slack, Spotify.  
- **Benchmark** – Small‑team firm that focuses on seed‑ and Series‑A rounds.  Portfolio: Uber, Twitter, Zillow, Snapchat.  
- **Kleiner Perkins (KP)** – Long‑standing backer of hardware, clean‑tech, and AI.  Portfolio: Amazon, Google, Twitter, NVIDIA.  
- **Greylock Partners** – Emphasis on consumer internet and enterprise SaaS.  Portfolio: LinkedIn, Airbnb, Dropbox, Workday.  
- **Lightspeed Venture Partners** – Strong in consumer, enterprise, and fintech.  Portfolio: Snap, Nest, AppDynamics, Rubrik.  
- **Founders Fund** – Known for “moonshot” bets in AI, aerospace, and biotech.  Portfolio: SpaceX, Palantir, Stripe, Lyft.  
- **IVP (Institutional Venture Partners)** – Focuses on later‑stage growth investments.  Portfolio: Snap, Slack, Dropbox, Twitter.  
- **Bessemer Venture Partners** – One of the world’s oldest firms; diversified across cloud, AI, and health‑tech.  Portfolio: Shopify, Twilio, Pinterest, LinkedIn.  
- **DFJ (Draper Fisher Jurvetson)** – Early investor in Tesla, SpaceX, and Amazon.  Portfolio now includes SpaceX, Unity, and Rocket Lab.  
- **NEA (New Enterprise Associates)** – While headquartered in Menlo Park and broader U.S., NEA is a leading mega‑fund with a substantial Silicon Valley footprint.  Portfolio: Coursera, Snowflake, Robinhood.  

#### Why These Firms Matter
- **Fund size & longevity**: Most manage tens of billions of dollars, allowing them to participate in multiple rounds and support companies through to IPO or acquisition.  
- **Track record**: The firms listed have backed several “unicorn” startups and have produced multiple high‑profile exits.  
- **Network effect**: Their brand alone can open doors to talent, customers, and later investors, giving portfolio companies a competitive advantage.  

If you’re looking for a venture partner, consider the stage you’re at (seed vs. growth), industry focus, and the specific partners within each firm who specialize in your market. Happy fundraising!
